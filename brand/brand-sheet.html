Rules

keep the split; change nothing else

Do keep the three-pane proportion and the 24-unit gutters.

Don't add a camera, aperture, photo stack or rounded photo cards.

Do use the light mark on paper/white, the dark mark on ink/#0a0a0a.

Don't outline the panes, add strokes, shadows, gradients or a fourth pane.

Do use the small cut below 24 px; use the tile icons for app/favicon.

Don't stretch, rotate, or change the 54/58 split.
